Roadmap

Evidence before expansion.

Jethos progresses through capability gates and acceptance criteria rather than promising dates before dependencies are understood.

Today

Validate the foundation

Private USDC Conservative PoC, supply-only integrations, accounting reconciliation, withdrawal recovery, monitoring and incident procedures.

Exit criteria

  • No unexplained accounting differences.
  • Normal withdrawal paths reproduce expected results.
  • Automation respects approved policy.
  • Pause and recovery procedures are repeatable.
Next

Expand the app and vault families

Consolidate the self-custodial experience, risk methodology and additional asset/profile combinations.

Dependencies

  • PoC acceptance criteria met.
  • Product policy approved.
  • Vault-specific tests and disclosures.
  • Independent review before public capital.
Later

Compose and scale

Independent EVM deployments and progressively composable vaults.

Dependencies

  • DAG/cycle controls.
  • Look-through exposure accounting.
  • Chain-specific certification.
  • Liquidity and withdrawal model.
Vision

Complete the financial home

Potential payments, cards, credit and tokenized assets integrated through the right technical and regulated partners.
