Why

DeFi is powerful, but fragmented.

Users navigate wallets, chains, markets, bridges, rewards, liquidations and interfaces to perform activities that should be understandable from one place.

Traditional financial interfaces are familiar but rarely expose a real-time, verifiable map of how deposited capital is used.

Jethos direction

Combine the ownership model of self-custody with the usability of digital banking and the transparency of public infrastructure.

This direction does not make Jethos a regulated bank and does not turn smart contracts into insured deposits.
