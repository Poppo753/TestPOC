How it works

Control before yield.

What stays in your wallet stays under your control. Only an explicit approval and deposit move capital across the boundary into a Jethos vault.

Two states

Available is not invested.

Wallet assets are outside the vault. Once deposited, capital follows smart-contract logic, approved integrations and documented administrative controls.

Self-custody is a responsibility. A compromised key, malicious signature or token approval can still put wallet assets at risk.

Vault shares

What Jethos can do

  • Use assets explicitly deposited in a vault.
  • Allocate through authorized modules.
  • Apply configured fees, limits and pauses.
  • Expose positions and transactions for verification.

What Jethos should not imply

  • Control over unrelated wallet assets.
  • Guaranteed returns or principal.
  • Immediate liquidity in every condition.
  • Absence of smart-contract or admin risk.
