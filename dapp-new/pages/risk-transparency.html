What risk analysis must cover

Smart contracts

Audits, maturity, incidents, upgrades and bug bounties.

Markets

TVL, utilization, liquidity and concentration.

Assets & oracles

Depeg, wrappers, issuers, staleness and manipulation.

Operations

Keys, monitoring, replay and emergency procedures.

Risk cannot be eliminated. Smart contracts, protocols, assets, oracles, sequencers and operational systems can fail. Liquidity can disappear.
