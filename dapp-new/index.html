The capital boundary · six checks

Nothing crosses it silently.

Six distinct checks follow capital from wallet ownership to withdrawal: who controls it, what is requested, what gets signed, how the position is represented, where it is allocated and under which conditions it can return.

01Wallet ownershipYour recovery phrase secures the wallet. Jethos neither stores it nor can recreate it.
02Explicit intentYou choose the asset, exact amount, requested action and destination before continuing.
03Wallet authorizationThe wallet shows what will be signed. Nothing moves without a valid confirmation.
04Vault positionOnly the deposited amount enters vault rules; shares represent the resulting economic position.
05Visible allocationEligible integrations, current routes, costs and material dependencies should remain inspectable.
06WithdrawalYou request the exit; timing and amount remain subject to contract state, reserves and available liquidity.
